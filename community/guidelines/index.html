Our shared commitments · Launch draft

Make room for people.
Care for their trust.

These guidelines describe the communities we’re preparing. Membership and posting are not open yet. Our initial communities are intended for adults age 18 and older.

1. Speak from your own experience

Say what helped you without assuming it is right for every family. Ask whether someone wants ideas, encouragement, or simply to be heard. Differences in culture, disability, family structure, and care choices belong here.

2. Protect each person’s dignity and privacy

Use a display name if you prefer. Avoid names, addresses, school or facility details, records, or photographs that identify a loved one. Speak respectfully about the person you support, including when a situation is difficult. Members-only does not mean confidential: others may copy or share a post.

3. Offer support within its limits

Do not diagnose people, prescribe treatment, or tell someone to change medication. Share resources with their source and distinguish personal experience from professional guidance. For urgent danger or an emergency, contact local emergency services; do not wait for a forum reply.

4. Keep the space welcoming

No harassment, hate, threats, exploitation, spam, or unsolicited selling. Professionals must disclose relevant affiliations and may not use the community to recruit clients. Do not copy another member’s story into marketing or other spaces without their permission.

5. No solicitation or self-promotion

Do not advertise your business, recruit clients, send unsolicited promotional messages, share commercial affiliate links, or use other members’ contact details for marketing without written permission from Care & Stay. Disguised sales pitches and repeated recommendations of your own services are not allowed. Professionals are welcome to help, but must disclose relevant affiliations and not treat discussions as sales leads. Genuine recommendations of unaffiliated services are welcome; disclose any financial connection or personal benefit. Promotional posts can be removed and members may be suspended or permanently removed, including immediately when necessary to protect the community. Any Care & Stay-approved sponsorship will be clearly identified.

6. Help us care for the community

At launch, reporting and moderator review must be available. Moderators may remove content, pause participation, or suspend accounts when needed. Moderation will not be continuous or an emergency response service. Reporting routes and response expectations will be published before posting opens.

How we’ll use AI

Any AI-generated community content will be labeled. AI will not impersonate members or create fictional personal experiences. Human moderators will remain responsible for moderation decisions.

Before member registration opens

We will publish the community privacy notice and membership terms, including account deletion, content handling, reporting, and moderation arrangements.
