Free community membership terms — draft

Care & Stay plans to offer two distinct, free, members-only peer communities: Family Caregivers and DD Parents & Families. These forums are not dependent on purchasing a Care & Stay app or assessment.

Who may participate

Members must be 18 or older, use an account they control, verify the email address associated with it, and affirm the community guidelines. Membership in one forum does not automatically grant access to another. Members may use a display name but may not impersonate others.

Peer support, not clinical or emergency services

Community posts reflect personal experiences and views of their authors, not professional medical, legal or financial advice. Posts are not monitored continuously. For an emergency, seek immediate local emergency assistance rather than posting in a forum.

Respect privacy and community safety

Do not publish identifiable details or records about people in your care. Harassment, threats, spam, exploitation, unauthorized solicitation, and using member stories for marketing without consent are prohibited. The full guidelines apply to every member.

No solicitation or self-promotion

Care & Stay Communities are peer-support spaces, not marketplaces for products or services. Unless Care & Stay gives prior written permission, members must not advertise, promote, solicit business, recruit clients, share affiliate or referral links for commercial gain, or collect members’ contact details for marketing. This includes unsolicited outreach to members, disguised advertisements, repeated recommendations of one’s own business, and offers framed as personal support primarily to generate sales.

Professionals and service providers may participate by sharing useful experience and resources, provided they disclose any relevant financial or professional affiliation and do not use discussions to prospect for customers. Members may make genuine, unsolicited-independent recommendations of services they have found helpful, provided they disclose any personal or commercial connection and do not receive undisclosed benefits.

Violations may lead to removal of promotional content, restricted participation, suspension, or permanent termination of forum membership, including immediate restriction where necessary to protect other members. Care & Stay may permit clearly labeled sponsorships or educational partnerships separately; such arrangements do not grant permission to solicit members in discussions.

Moderation

Moderators may hide content, review reported discussions, and temporarily or permanently restrict community access when needed for safety or rule enforcement. Community members can report concerning content. Final review schedules and a supported appeal/contact process must be established before launch.

Member control

Members may remove their own posts and leave a community through their account controls. Complete account closure requires an assisted process and identity verification. Other members may already have copied material; removing a post cannot recall those copies.

Payment and promotion

Basic community membership is free. Separate Care & Stay applications and courses may carry fees, and clearly labeled relevant promotions or sponsorships may appear in public areas. Participation in discussions does not require a purchase or consent to marketing email.

Before these terms take effect

These are draft terms for review only. The final version, support contact, retention practices, and any required notices will be published before new members are invited.

This is a working draft.

Care & Stay has not yet approved the final retention periods, verified its support inbox, or completed its launch review. Public registration must remain closed until those items are finalized.
