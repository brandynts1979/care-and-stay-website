Community privacy notice — draft

This notice describes the planned operation of the free Care & Stay discussion communities for adults. The public community launch has not occurred.

Information members may provide

Registration uses an email address and authentication credentials. Members select a display name and the community or communities they join. Participation may generate posts, replies, reports, moderation records and account activity needed to operate and protect the forums. We ask members not to publish identifying medical, school, care, family or financial records about themselves or others.

How the information is used

To provide account access and discussions; verify membership; investigate safety and conduct reports; prevent abuse; support account and content requests; and maintain service security. Care & Stay does not require a paid subscription for basic forum membership.

Who may see content

Other signed-in members of the community where a post is made can read that post. Authorized moderators can review reports and apply forum rules. Members may copy what they see; the forum cannot guarantee confidentiality. The two communities are separated by membership and moderation permissions.

Service providers and marketing

Care & Stay currently uses Supabase for the community authentication and database and Render to host the staging site. Other service providers and their retention terms should be reviewed before launch. Forum registration does not itself subscribe anyone to promotional email. Any optional newsletter or marketing email must have separate consent and an unsubscribe method. We do not plan to sell member email lists or use private posts for targeted advertising.

Content removal and account closure

Members can remove their own messages and leave individual communities. Complete forum-account closure currently requires a request to the support team and identity verification; no self-service full account deletion is offered. Removal can hide threads and their replies from regular forum views, but cannot undo copies made by others or immediately erase provider backups and logs.

Storage and retention

Discussion content and moderation records are retained under the operating policy that is still being finalized. No 90-day purge schedule has yet been activated, and no backup-erasure time is promised. Final retention periods and procedures must be confirmed and published before open registration.

Contact

Proposed support address, pending mailbox verification: hello@mycareandstay.com. Do not send emergency or sensitive care records through the community.

This is a working draft.

Care & Stay has not yet approved the final retention periods, verified its support inbox, or completed its launch review. Public registration must remain closed until those items are finalized.
