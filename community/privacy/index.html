Community privacy notice

This notice explains how Care & Stay handles data for its free, members-only discussion communities for adults. Registration may be temporarily closed during launch preparation.

Information members may provide

Registration uses an email address and authentication credentials. Members select a display name and the community or communities they join. Participation may generate posts, replies, reports, moderation records and account activity needed to operate and protect the forums. We ask members not to publish identifying medical, school, care, family or financial records about themselves or others.

How the information is used

To provide account access and discussions; verify membership; investigate safety and conduct reports; prevent abuse; support account and content requests; and maintain service security. Care & Stay does not require a paid subscription for basic forum membership.

Who may see content

Other signed-in members of the community where a post is made can read that post. Authorized moderators can review reports and apply forum rules. Members may copy what they see; the forum cannot guarantee confidentiality. The two communities are separated by membership and moderation permissions.

Service providers and marketing

Care & Stay uses Supabase for community authentication and database storage and Render for website hosting. These providers process technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Forum registration does not itself subscribe anyone to promotional email. Any optional newsletter or marketing email must have separate consent and an unsubscribe method. We do not plan to sell member email lists or use private posts for targeted advertising.

Content removal and account closure

Members can remove their own messages and leave individual communities. Complete forum-account closure currently requires a request to the support team and identity verification; no self-service full account deletion is offered. Removal can hide threads and their replies from regular forum views, but cannot undo copies made by others or immediately erase provider backups and logs.

Storage and retention

Member discussions remain while the communities operate unless removed by a member or moderator. Resolved reports and routine moderation audit records are eligible for automatic deletion after 90 days. Records under an investigation or documented preservation hold are exempt until the hold is released. Unresolved reports are not deleted under this schedule. Database backups are subject to the provider's retention procedures, and deletion from the active database may not immediately erase historical backup copies.

Contact

For community support, membership questions, and assisted account-closure requests, contact hello@mycareandstay.com. Do not send emergency or sensitive care records through the community.

Privacy reminder: Do not post identifying, medical, school, or financial records. Moderation is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency response service.
