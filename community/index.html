A few things to know

Can I post or create an account yet?

Not yet. These pages introduce the communities. Member registration, discussions, and moderation are still being prepared.

Will discussions be public?

Our planned model is members-only discussion, separate from public articles. Members may still copy or share what they read, so avoid posting identifying details about loved ones.

How can I help shape the community?

Email us about the founding group. Tell us which community interests you and what would make it useful. Please leave out private care details.
