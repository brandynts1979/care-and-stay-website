How this estimate is calculated

For each employee role, we multiply the number of avoidable departures per year by the cost of replacing one employee. We then add the three amounts to estimate your annual replacement-cost exposure.

For example, three hourly departures at $2,706 per replacement produce an estimate of $8,118.

What goes into turnover costs?

The industry benchmarks cover separation, replacement, and training. When estimating your own costs, consider the following activities.

  • Separation. Staff time spent processing a departure, completing exit administration, and arranging the handover.
  • Replacement. Recruiting expenses and staff time spent advertising, reviewing applications, interviewing, and completing hiring checks.
  • Training. Onboarding materials, paid training time, and the time managers or experienced employees spend teaching a replacement.

These examples can help you estimate your own costs. The cited source reports combined costs for these categories without an itemized dollar breakdown for each activity.

Broader effects can include disrupted service, lost sales, and strain on team morale. This calculator does not assign additional dollar amounts to those effects. Its total is based entirely on the departure counts and replacement costs entered above.
