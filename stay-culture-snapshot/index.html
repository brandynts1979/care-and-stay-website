What these results mean

Three appreciative next steps

  1. Invite a colleague to share a story of when your organization was at its best. Ask what made that experience possible.
  2. Explore your lowest-scoring pillar with curiosity: what already works in this area, and what could make it more consistent?
  3. Choose one small, observable leadership practice to try over the next 30 days and invite feedback.

Each pillar is based on just two items answered by one leader. Differences of a few tenths should not be treated as statistically meaningful. These results are for reflection, not benchmarking or predicting turnover.
