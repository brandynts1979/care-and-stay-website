Stay Culture Journal Newsletter Privacy Notice

Care & Stay LLC
Effective date: To be assigned on publication

This notice explains how Care & Stay LLC intends to handle information submitted to the free Stay Culture Journal email subscription form. It applies to this newsletter only; other Care & Stay services, such as caregiver apps, assessments, or community accounts, may involve additional information and separate disclosures.

Information collected

The newsletter form requests an email address and may also collect an optional first name, optional selections of topics that interest you, and your newsletter consent choices. HubSpot may also process form submission metadata needed to operate and protect its service; the precise metadata and cookie practices must be verified before this notice becomes effective.

Why we use it

We use newsletter subscription information to deliver the Stay Culture Journal, which is planned to include two feature articles each month plus occasional shorter editorial insights, and to manage your communication preferences. Interest selections may help us tailor topics. We do not treat an unrelated CRM contact or sales lead as an opted-in Journal reader.

Email service provider

The signup form is configured in HubSpot, which will process subscription details and communication preferences on our behalf. We may use other technical service providers to host the Journal website. We do not offer personal newsletter details for sale.

Your choices

Subscribing is voluntary. You can stop receiving Journal marketing emails at any time through an unsubscribe link in each email. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you supplied for this newsletter, email hello@mycareandstay.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited records where needed to honor an unsubscribe request or comply with legal obligations.

Retention and security

Newsletter information is kept while needed for subscriptions and applicable recordkeeping. After unsubscribing, information may be retained to prevent unwanted resubscriptions and to meet legal requirements. We use service-provider and organizational safeguards appropriate to this activity, but no online service is completely risk-free. Specific retention periods and HubSpot account settings must be verified before approval.

Website technologies

The Journal website and embedded signup may use technical storage or cookies to operate forms and help prevent abuse. If optional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are activated, we will update the disclosures and choices as required. Actual tools and consent settings are subject to verification.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes materially, we will update this page and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, write to hello@mycareandstay.com.
