Stay Culture Journal Newsletter Privacy Notice

Care & Stay LLC
Effective date: November 14, 2026
Privacy contact: hello@mycareandstay.com

This notice explains how Care & Stay LLC handles information submitted to the free Stay Culture Journal email subscription form. It applies to this newsletter only; other Care & Stay services, such as caregiver apps, assessments, or community accounts, may involve additional information and separate disclosures.

Information collected

If you subscribe, we collect your email address, and, if you provide them, your first name and topic interests. We also process your consent selections and information associated with the form submission. HubSpot, our form service provider, may collect technical information such as browser and device details, IP address, cookies, and submission timestamps to provide and secure the service. The precise technical information depends on your browser and HubSpot settings.

Why we use it

We use newsletter subscription information to deliver the Stay Culture Journal, which is planned to include two feature articles each month plus occasional shorter editorial insights, and to manage your communication preferences. Interest selections may help us tailor topics. We do not treat an unrelated CRM contact or sales lead as an opted-in Journal reader.

Email service provider

We use HubSpot to process newsletter signup details and communication preferences. Our website is hosted using Render. We do not sell newsletter subscriber information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the security or integrity of our services.

Your choices

Subscribing is voluntary. You can stop receiving Journal marketing emails at any time through an unsubscribe link in each email. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you supplied for this newsletter, email hello@mycareandstay.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited records where needed to honor an unsubscribe request or comply with legal obligations.

Retention and security

Newsletter information is kept while needed for subscriptions and applicable recordkeeping. After unsubscribing, information may be retained to prevent unwanted resubscriptions and to meet legal requirements. We use service-provider and organizational safeguards appropriate to this activity, but no online service is completely risk-free. We review and delete information when it is no longer needed for these purposes, subject to lawful preservation needs and service-provider backup processes.

Website technologies

The Journal website and HubSpot form may use cookies or similar technologies to display and operate the form, remember user preferences, help prevent abuse, and collect service diagnostics. You may control certain cookies through your browser settings. We will update this notice if we add materially different analytics or advertising activities.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes materially, we will update this page and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, write to hello@mycareandstay.com.
