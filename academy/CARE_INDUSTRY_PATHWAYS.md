# Care industry leadership pathways — development specification

Status: planned; not enrolled or deployed as learning functionality.

Reuse the existing academy authentication, learner progress, organization membership and shared leadership curriculum. Preserve existing QSR enrollment and lessons. Add organization-assigned pathway keys qsr, home_care, dd_services; enforce authorization server-side. Keep core concepts shared, with contextual scenarios, exercises, reflections and assessments per pathway. Target 70% shared and 30% contextual, subject to pilot feedback.

## Home care
1. Servant leadership with distributed caregivers
2. Trust and psychological safety in field care
3. Coaching and clear performance expectations
4. Difficult conversations with families and caregivers
5. Conflict transformation and professional boundaries
6. Recognition, resilience and retention
7. Appreciative quality improvement
8. Leadership action plan

## Developmental disability services
1. Dignity, choice and self-determination
2. Psychological safety and speaking up
3. Coaching direct support professionals
4. Difficult conversations about autonomy and support
5. Conflict transformation among individuals, families and staff
6. Professional boundaries and supported decision-making
7. Appreciative inquiry and quality-of-life outcomes
8. Leadership action plan

Each lesson: substantive core reading, contextual case, reflection, practice conversation, knowledge check and practical action. AI simulation uses fictional cases only, no personal care records; feedback addresses listening, curiosity, respect, clarity and accountability. Do not market as mandated clinical or regulatory training. Escalate real safeguarding concerns according to agency policy; do not substitute AI for required reporting.

## Acceptance criteria
- Existing QSR progress unaffected.
- Owners invite managers into their assigned pathway; no cross-organization access.
- Each pathway has meaningful contextual learning, accessible navigation and saved reflections.
- Test invitation, completion, progress and role boundaries before release.
- Existing Individual 360 and Stay Culture 360 may inform development plans only with appropriate authorization.

## Pilot
Recruit local home care and DD agency leaders; measure baseline and follow-up leadership confidence, practice application, engagement and qualitative feedback. No guaranteed retention or quality improvement claims.
