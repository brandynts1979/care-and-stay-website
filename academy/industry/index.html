One leadership foundation, relevant workplace applications

The planned program includes eight modules and 32 foundational lessons per Academy, formative decision scenarios and knowledge checks, practical worksheets, reflection and guided leadership practice. Each Academy has its own course identity. Universal video resources and personalized learning features are subject to final verification.

  • Authentic and servant leadership
  • Emotional intelligence
  • Appreciative communication and listening
  • Coaching and talent development
  • Conflict transformation
  • Courageous conversations and compassionate accountability
  • Trust, belonging and psychological safety
  • Empowerment and sustainable leadership

Leadership education only. Not clinical instruction, continuing education credit, licensure or regulatory certification. No guaranteed employment, retention or care outcomes.
