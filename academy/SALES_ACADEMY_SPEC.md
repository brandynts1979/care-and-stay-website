# Stay Culture Sales & Business Development Academy

Status: proposed curriculum and implementation specification; no enrollment or billing changes.

## Strategy
Leadership Academy is the flagship entry product, grounded in the existing Seven Pillars of a Stay Culture and eight proposed leadership competencies. Sales Academy is a complementary entry product sharing the learning infrastructure but with independent enrollment, progress and curriculum. Preserve existing QSR courses. Offer coaching, 360 assessments, team development and Appreciative Inquiry summits as optional follow-on services.

## Eight sales competencies (four lessons each)
1. Appreciative business development: ethical consultative selling; curiosity; customer-defined value; trusted resource positioning.
2. Market intelligence and segmentation: referral ecosystem; ideal partners; niches and micro-niches; territory planning.
3. Pipeline development: prospect research; outreach cadence; qualification; pipeline metrics and forecasting.
4. Discovery and open-ended questions: current successes; unmet needs; desired outcomes; reflective listening and summarizing.
5. Co-creating value: stakeholder mapping; shared solution design; service feasibility; mutually agreed next steps.
6. Experimentation and learning: hypothesis; small outreach tests; measurement; iteration with appropriate comparison and sample-size caution.
7. Relationship and referral development: follow-up; educational value; trust recovery; account expansion.
8. Sustainable performance: weekly routines; conversion analysis; ethical commitments and handoff; 90-day growth plan.

## Industry tracks
Home care, hospice, home health, assisted living/senior living, developmental disability services, and general B2B. Shared concepts with tailored scenarios, buyer roles and vocabulary. Do not encourage referral inducements, patient steering, pressure, unsupported claims or promises outside clinical/operational capacity. Respect patient choice, eligibility and applicable referral/privacy rules. Use fictional scenarios without identifiable care data.

## Lesson format
Substantive reading, realistic case, open-ended questions, practice exercise, reflection, knowledge check, measurable field experiment and optional AI role-play with feedback. No clinical or compliance certification claims.

## Technical requirements
Reuse secure academy learning infrastructure, server-authorized course entitlements and progress. Separate course identifiers and versioned curriculum; avoid changing existing QSR enrollment or billing. Test tenant isolation, invitation, completion and access revocation before launch. Sales and leadership bundles require separately approved pricing and entitlements.

## Pilot metrics
Qualified partner conversations, discovery-to-next-step conversion, appropriate referrals, referral source diversification, pipeline coverage, follow-up reliability, and learning application. Track baselines; do not guarantee revenue or referrals.
