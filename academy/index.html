Lessons use scenarios, reflection, practical assignments, and feedback. Videos are optional additions and are not required to begin or finish the text-based learning experience.

Can learners start without videos?

Yes. The planned launch experience is complete text-based learning. Videos may be added later.

Are personal reflections shared with supervisors?

Private reflections are intended for the learner alone. Only separately submitted practice evidence is available to assigned reviewers.

Can an owner invite unlimited managers?

Yes. Each subscribed restaurant location includes an unlimited number of managers and shift leaders. The owner may authorize a GM to manage invitations.
