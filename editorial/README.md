# Stay Culture Journal — Editorial workflow

Status: EDITORIAL WORK IN PROGRESS. No content in this directory is approved for publication.

## Decisions confirmed
- Publication: **Stay Culture Journal**, housed within Care & Stay at `/stay-culture-journal/`.
- Access: publicly readable, free articles.
- Approval: AI may research and draft, but **all publications require explicit owner approval**.
- Cadence: two feature articles per month, with shorter insights between features.
- The preexisting community-and-journal-foundation branch already contains a Journal homepage and a draft, "The Conversation Before the Resignation". Preserve it and do not duplicate it.

## Editorial categories
1. Leadership & Coaching
2. Stay Culture & Retention
3. Leadership in Practice (senior care, hospice, home care, QSR, franchising, education)
4. Conversations & Interviews
5. Research & Insights
6. Reflections on Becoming

## Content acquisition
- Original leadership essays developed from the founder's frameworks, anonymized experiences, and interviews.
- Reliable primary research: BLS/JOLTS, Gallup workplace, AHRQ, peer-reviewed journals; verify every empirical claim against the primary source.
- Guest contributions only with express permission and editorial review.
- Care & Stay 360 data only when properly authorized, adequately aggregated and privacy-safe; avoid unsupported statistical claims or identifiable participant details.
- Never fabricate interviews, outcomes, case studies, quotes, or numbers.
- Paraphrase with attribution and link to original research, do not republish copyrighted content without permission.

## Publication workflow
Idea -> Research notes & sources -> Draft -> Fact-check -> Owner approval -> Production staging -> Publish.
Only the owner may authorize the transition from draft to approved. No auto-publishing and no auto-email sends.
Do not merge this editorial branch to community staging or production until the owner approves both the articles and their appearance.
