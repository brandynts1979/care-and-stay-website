# Stay Culture Journal — Short Research Insight Template
Status: internal editorial template only; never publish without owner approval.

**Working title:** [Specific, non-sensational statement]
**Category:** Research & Insights
**Target length:** 250–450 words
**Evidence checked:** [Date]
**Original primary source:** [Author/organization, exact title, publication date, URL]
**Review status:** Draft → Fact checked → Owner approved

## The finding
One or two sentences about what the underlying original research actually found. Explain sample, population, and geography if relevant. Do not invent percentages or generalize beyond the study.

## Why it matters
Describe an actionable implication for senior care, QSR or other leaders. Separate evidence from Care & Stay interpretation.

## Put it into practice
One specific experiment, conversation or question leaders can try this week.

## Reflection
One appreciative, open-ended question.

## Sources and editorial notes
Link primary research and any qualifying limitations, including whether the findings show correlation rather than causation. Cite and attribute; no copied proprietary charts, long quotes or full-text republication.

## Suggested next step
Link to the most relevant Care & Stay resource only if useful: free snapshot, organizational 360, leadership academy, or coaching.

**Publishing controls:** Owner approval required for all insights, headlines, distribution emails, and social posts. No fabricated quotes, interviews or outcomes.
