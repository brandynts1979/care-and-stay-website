1. Information we collect

Depending on the services you use, Care & Stay may receive contact details, newsletter preferences, account information, assessment submissions, community content, and information you choose to provide to caregiver tools. 2. How information is used

Potential uses to verify include responding to inquiries, providing subscribed Journal emails, operating accounts and purchased services, generating assessments and reports, preventing misuse, supporting customer service, and improving services. 3. Newsletter communications

Journal subscription is voluntary. The intended experience is two feature articles per month plus occasional shorter editorial insights. Readers should be able to unsubscribe from marketing email. The final policy must explain how consent is captured, stored, and honored, including HubSpot's role.

4. Caregiving, assessment, and community content

Some Care & Stay services may involve particularly personal information. The final policy must distinguish private caregiver records, employer-level and individual assessment responses, community posts, and public Journal readership.
