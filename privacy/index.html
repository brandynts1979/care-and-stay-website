Care & Stay Privacy Policy

Business: Care & Stay LLC
Privacy contact: hello@mycareandstay.com

Effective date: To be determined following approval.

This draft is intended to cover Care & Stay's website, Stay Culture Journal and newsletter, caregiver and developmental-disability family services, leadership assessments, and community spaces. Care & Stay LLC is the business responsible for this draft. The final version must accurately identify the services it operates and any service-specific terms.

1. Information we collect

Care & Stay's connected product databases include profile and care-recipient records; medications, allergies, care needs, care plans, family-team members, authorizations, financial records, and documents; organizational assessment invitations, responses, and AI reports; individual leadership 360 engagements, rater nominations, drafts, and AI review records; and developmental-disability family planning records, including strengths, goals, progress notes, team invitations, and documents. The Journal newsletter form under development is intended to collect email address, optional first name, optional topic interests, and communication consent. The final policy must verify which categories are actually collected in production, what additional information is stored by providers, and what is optional.

2. How information is used

Potential uses to verify include responding to inquiries, providing subscribed Journal emails, operating accounts and purchased services, generating assessments and reports, preventing misuse, supporting customer service, and improving services. The final policy must identify each actual use and any relevant consent choices.

3. Newsletter communications

Journal subscription is voluntary. The intended experience is two feature articles per month plus occasional shorter editorial insights. Readers should be able to unsubscribe from marketing email. The planned HubSpot form includes separate consent for marketing communications. Its subscription type currently uses HubSpot's broad Marketing Information category. Before launch, confirm how the form records consent, unsubscribes, and selected interests, and whether recipients may receive other marketing under that subscription type. No Journal subscription form has been activated through this draft-policy work.

4. Caregiving, assessment, and community content

Some Care & Stay services may involve particularly personal information. The final policy must distinguish private caregiver records, employer-level and individual assessment responses, community posts, and public Journal readership. In the connected Supabase projects inspected on November 13, 2026, row-level security was enabled on the listed public data tables. This does not establish that every policy, privileged function, or storage rule is safe or complete. The final policy must accurately describe access controls, confidentiality, participant reporting thresholds, retention, and any AI processing, aligned with tested implementations and contractual commitments.

5. Service providers and sharing

Connected systems reviewed include Render for website and app deployment, Supabase for app database infrastructure, and HubSpot for contact and marketing workflows. Stripe is used in the broader Care & Stay payment setup, but the specific active payment flows and personal information exchanged still require verification. AI-assisted assessment/reporting features also require verification of the AI provider, data fields transmitted, and retention practices. The final policy must distinguish deployed integrations from planned ones and accurately describe the actual service providers, their roles, purposes, and safeguards. Do not assume a provider handles a particular category of personal data until verified.

6. Cookies and analytics

The final policy must explain which cookies, analytics tools, tracking pixels, and marketing technologies are actually in use, along with any applicable choices and notices.

7. Information security and retention

Security measures, deletion methods, backup retention, data retention periods, and access practices must be documented accurately. No guarantee of absolute security should be made.

8. Your privacy choices

Explain how people can request access, correction, deletion, or other rights applicable to them; how they can unsubscribe; and what verification may be required. Confirm the appropriate request address and procedure.

9. Children and family accounts

The final policy must state the intended user ages, whether parents can store information about children or dependents, and any applicable protections. Do not treat a parent-managed dependent profile as independent consent from a minor.

10. Changes and contact

Describe how changes to the policy will be communicated. The responsible business is Care & Stay LLC. Privacy inquiries may be directed to hello@mycareandstay.com, subject to confirmation of the privacy-request procedure. No owner's home or personal mailing address will be published in this policy. Confirm whether any jurisdiction-specific disclosures require another contact method before approval.
