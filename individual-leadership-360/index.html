A thoughtful approach

Know what your results mean.

Will the report identify blind spots or hidden strengths?

It highlights differences worth exploring. When others rate a behavior higher than you do, there may be an under-recognized strength. When you rate yourself higher, differences in expectations or context may be worth discussing. A rating gap alone does not prove a blind spot or establish how accurate your self-awareness is.

Are there external benchmarks?

Scores use a 1–5 behavior-frequency scale and compare eligible perspectives within your assessment. We do not currently apply validated external norms, percentiles, or an overall leadership score.

How is feedback protected?

Peer and Direct report numeric results require at least three distinct completed respondents in each group, with enough observations for each competency. Smaller results stay withheld. Manager feedback is identifiable by role. Written examples can contain identifying context, so respondents should avoid names and unnecessary identifying details; the coach reviews eligible excerpts before approving AI drafting.

Who receives the report?

The coach reviews and approves the report before coach-led delivery. Approval does not automatically send it. Agree with your coach before starting on who will receive it and how it will be used.

Is this a validated diagnostic or hiring test?

No. This is a developmental assessment designed to support reflection and coaching. It has not been psychometrically validated and should not be used as a diagnostic or employment selection test.
