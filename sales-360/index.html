Thoughtful, confidential feedback

Insight without compromising trust.

What feedback will I receive?

Your self-ratings and pooled feedback from eligible other respondents. Other-respondent scores require at least three independent completed respondents with sufficient observations for each competency. Insufficient results remain unavailable.

Is the assessment anonymous?

No. Responses are confidential rather than anonymous. Verified accounts and assignments support access control. Individual respondent identities and raw feedback are not included in participant reports.

Who can see my report?

Only authorized access is permitted. A coach must explicitly release your own aggregate report before you can view or print it. A sponsor does not receive individual reports or raw responses as part of the team pilot.

Can this be used for hiring or discipline?

No. Sales 360 is a developmental assessment, not a diagnostic, validated selection instrument or guarantee of business growth. Please avoid patient names, clinical details and unnecessary personal information.

How do I get started?

Contact Care & Stay to discuss fit, the feedback group, coaching scope, privacy and scheduling. If you have been invited to participate, follow the private instructions from your coach. Do not send passwords, assessment responses or one-time invitation codes by email.
