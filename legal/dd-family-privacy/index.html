DD Family Care Privacy Notice

Effective November 15, 2026. This notice describes intended information practices for the DD Family Care application. Its production release is pending verification; the current demonstration is restricted to fictional information.

Information handled

Account contact details and sign-in information; family care profiles, preferences and strengths; plans, goals, tasks, supporters, health/education/therapy/benefits records and documents that authorized account holders choose to enter; and operational, security and billing records. Do not provide real sensitive information in the development demonstration.

Purposes

To operate family-care planning, coordinate permissioned access, manage paid subscriptions, provide support, secure accounts, diagnose errors and meet legal obligations. We do not sell care profile contents.

Providers and sharing

The service uses Supabase for authentication and data storage, Render for hosting and Stripe for subscription billing. Access can be shared with invited supporters subject to available app permissions. Planned AI features may involve an additional AI service provider; do not assume AI features are available until clearly described at release.

Security, correction and deletion

Accounts use sign-in and application-level access controls. No online service guarantees absolute security. Contact us to request information, corrections or deletion. Legal, billing or security retention requirements may apply. Do not rely on the app as the sole copy of critical emergency information.

Children and people receiving support

Accounts are for adults. Information about a child or adult receiving support may only be added by a person with appropriate permission or authority. Do not register a child for an independent account.

Contact

Email hello@mycareandstay.com with the subject “DD Family Care privacy request.”
