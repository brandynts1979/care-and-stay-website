DD Family Care Subscription Terms

Effective November 15, 2026. These terms apply when paid DD Family Care subscriptions become available. No paid DD subscription is currently available through this page. They supplement the terms displayed at checkout.

Pricing and commitment

Approved planned subscription price: $39 per month plus applicable sales tax, with a minimum initial 12-month commitment (12 payments totaling $468 before tax). Payments would be collected monthly from the authorized payment method. After the initial commitment, the subscription continues month-to-month unless canceled. The checkout terms and total will be disclosed before payment.

Cancellation and refunds

Contact hello@mycareandstay.com to request cancellation or nonrenewal. During the first 12 months, a cancellation request does not automatically waive remaining payments unless required by law. Payments are generally nonrefundable except as required by applicable law and payment-provider rules. Do not assume cancellation automatically deletes stored information.

Scope of the service

DD Family Care helps adult family caregivers organize strengths-based goals, preferences, care information, tasks, records, and transition planning for children or adults with developmental disabilities. It is not emergency assistance or medical, mental-health, educational, legal, or benefits advice. Features labeled planned or in development are not included as present functionality.

Authority and privacy

Adult account holders must have appropriate consent or legal authority to enter information about another person and to invite supporters. Keep credentials secure and use the service lawfully. See the DD Family Care Privacy Notice. Contact us for billing, accessibility or account questions.
