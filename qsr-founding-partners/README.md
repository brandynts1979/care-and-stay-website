# Care & Stay QSR Founding Partner Program

Status: **Recruiting interest; paid launch gated** (November 24, 2026).

## Founding offer

- **$99 USD per restaurant location per month**, plus any applicable sales tax.
- **Includes all three offerings**: Stay Culture 360, Individual Leadership 360 and Stay Culture Leadership Academy.
- **Leadership Academy seats:** unlimited eligible managers, assistant managers and shift leaders at that participating location.
- **Founding rate protection:** 12 months from each location's subscription start. This is a price guarantee, not automatically a 12-month minimum; the subscription term and cancellation/refund rules need a written agreement before checkout.
- **Scope:** included assessments and support will be specified clearly during onboarding; do not advertise unlimited Individual 360 assessments or coaching where not operationally established.
- **Delivery:** invitation-only and phased. Do not take payments until all three offerings can be properly provisioned and their essential workflows verified.
- **Partner role:** give candid input on manager needs, training workflows, retention barriers, and reporting; prioritize changes that are useful on the restaurant floor.

## 90-day beta operating rhythm

| When | Owner / GM activity | Care & Stay deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Confirm locations, leader roster, priorities, baseline turnover and goals | Kickoff and clear access / privacy instructions |
| First 30 days | Experience Academy, initial individual feedback and organization survey workflows | Collect usability issues and first improvement list |
| Days 31–60 | Give structured feedback on adoption and leader needs | Implement highest-value safe fixes and share changes |
| Days 61–90 | Discuss practical changes in leadership behaviors and employee experience | Results review, roadmap and recommendation |

**Metrics to monitor:** verified active users, invitations accepted, lesson completion, user feedback, assessment participation (subject to confidentiality thresholds), manager actions implemented, and restaurant-reported turnover/retention trends. Never promise a percentage reduction in turnover; small samples, seasonality and other factors affect interpretation.
