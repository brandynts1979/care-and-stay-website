## Release decision — Nov 13, 2026
**NO-GO for customer-paid/public real-data launch.** Actual signed-in Storage transfer and four core record-module tests now passed, but unrelated-account Storage REST denial, anti-malware scanning, backup/restore, complete privacy/accessibility/reliability review, full service workflows, AI coach, and approved Stripe checkout/tax registration remain outstanding. A public *informational* landing/waitlist without account signup, payment or sensitive-data intake could be considered separately but has not been built or approved here.

## Rules
- Never put actual user files or credentials in automated tests.
- Prefer transactional tests that roll back fixture data; for storage E2E, create disposable fictional files and delete them.
- A committed test is **not** a passing test until an execution result is verified.
- Keep the DD development app separate from the original caregiver app.
- No customer launch until mandatory security, privacy and reliability gates pass.
